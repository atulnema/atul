# Outreach Templates

Short, human and sent once. Adjust the wording to fit the reporter; never send these word for word to more than one person at the same outlet.

---

## Thank-you (after a published quote)

**Subject:** Thanks for including [Expert first name]

> Hi [Reporter first name],
>
> Just saw the piece on [topic] — thanks for including [Expert name]'s take. It came together really well.
>
> If you're working on anything around [expert's 2–3 topics] later, happy to help again on short notice.
>
> [Your name]

No link request. This one is purely for the relationship.

---

## Unlinked mention (reclamation)

Send only when the outlet links to other sources in its articles. Skip if it clearly never links out.

**Subject:** Quick note on your [topic] article

> Hi [Reporter first name],
>
> Thanks again for quoting [Expert name] in [article title]. Really appreciated.
>
> Small thing: would you be open to linking [Brand name] to [target URL]? It would help readers who want to follow up on [the specific point quoted]. Completely understand if that's not your outlet's policy.
>
> [Your name]

Rules: one email, no follow-up, and never mention SEO, dofollow or anchor text.

---

## Wrong or broken link (correction)

**Subject:** Small link fix in your [topic] article

> Hi [Reporter first name],
>
> Thanks for featuring [Expert name] in [article title]. The link to [Brand name] currently goes to [wrong/broken URL] — the correct one is [target URL].
>
> Thanks so much, and no rush.
>
> [Your name]
